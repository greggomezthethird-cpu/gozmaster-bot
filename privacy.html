Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

About GozMaster Uploader

GozMaster Uploader is a personal automation tool that uploads videos to my own YouTube channel (Goz Master). It is not a service offered to the public — it has no other users. It collects no data from anyone other than myself, and there is no data shared with or sold to third parties.

Google / YouTube Sign-in

GozMaster Uploader uses Google OAuth to access my YouTube channel. The sign-in token that authorizes YouTube uploads is managed by Composio, a third-party service I use to maintain the connection. Composio holds the token on my behalf; no other party receives it. Nothing is sold or shared.

You can review and revoke any application's access to your Google account at any time:
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

YouTube API Services

GozMaster Uploader uses the YouTube API Services. By using it, I agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data collected through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

Data Collected

  • No personal data from visitors to this website is collected.
  • No analytics, tracking scripts, or cookies are used on this site.
  • The only data handled by GozMaster Uploader is my own YouTube channel credentials and the video files I upload.
